# Voice rubric

Score your own option before returning it. Review mode does not score; it
quotes and diagnoses.

## Hard fail (any one fails the option)

- A pattern or word from the cut list that the author did not supply.
- A specific-sounding claim with no source in `facts.md`, the draft or the
  corpus.
- A manufactured typo or grammar slip.
- A CTA, hashtag or emoji the draft did not have and the author did not ask for.
- A motivational or aphorism ending.
- Private context: transcript artifacts, client names, meeting details.

## Voice check (0 to 2 each)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct opening | Warm-up lap | Gets there by line 2 | First line is the point |
| Specificity | Fog | One concrete detail | Concrete by line 3 |
| Compression | Padded or repeated | Some filler | Nothing to cut |
| Human rhythm | Even, templated | Some variation | Ragged, natural |
| Plain language | Corporate gloss | Mostly plain | Sounds spoken |
| Author's moves | None present | One, forced | Present where earned |
| Restraint | Fake certainty or cleverness | Slight reach | Claims match evidence |
| Ending | Lesson voice | Fine | Lands, or stops cleanly |

**Pass: 13 or more out of 16, with no hard fail.** 11 or 12: edit and rescore.
Under 11: rewrite from the draft, not from your option.

## Last questions

- Could a generic account have posted this?
- Is your framing flagged so the author can veto it?
- Did you keep their strongest line?
