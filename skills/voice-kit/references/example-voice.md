# Worked example: Sam Okafor, voice profile

**Fictional.** Sam is an invented product designer who posts about prototyping.
Every quote, date and statistic below is made up to show what a filled profile
looks like. Do not copy these moves into a real profile. Find the author's own.

Sample: 48 posts from one platform, Mar 2025 to Jun 2026, plus 30 short posts
from a second platform (Sep 2026). Every rate describes these samples only.
Rebuilt: 2026-09-14.

## The register

Sam writes like someone reporting back from the desk: a thing was tried this
morning, here is what happened, here is the screenshot. Posts usually start from
a prototype that just worked or just broke. Default, confirmed by Sam: lead with
that report. A slower explanatory register is for tutorials and threads, and
never replaces the report.

Recurring moods, each with one dated quote:

- **Delighted by a small detail.** "The hover state on this toggle took 40
  minutes and it was worth every one." (2026-03-11)
- **Honest about a dead end.** "Spent the afternoon on a drag-to-reorder that
  nobody asked for. Deleting it." (2026-05-02)
- **Curious about other people's process.** "How do you all decide when a
  prototype is done enough to test?" (2026-04-19)

## Signature moves

**1. The parenthetical aside (31% of posts).** A short bracketed comment that
admits something the main sentence glosses over. Rare before 2025 (4%).

> Shipped the onboarding prototype to five testers (two of them are
> cousins, still counts). (2026-02-08)

> The new spacing scale is 4, 8, 12, 16 (and 6, because one button insisted).
> (2026-06-21)

Limit: one per post at most. Never in the opening line.

**2. The before and after pair.** Two screenshots and one sentence on what
changed. The images carry the argument.

> Left is Monday. Right is Thursday, after removing three of the five steps.
> (2026-04-03)

> Before: a modal. After: an inline edit. Testers stopped asking where the save
> button went. (2026-05-27)

Limit: only when both images exist. Never describe a before and after in words
alone.

**3. Exact numbers, said casually.** A count, a duration or a version, stated
plainly: "about 40 minutes", "six testers", "v0.3".

> Took 25 minutes from sketch to clickable, mostly because I stopped
> picking fonts. (2026-01-30)

> Third round of testing, seven people, and nobody found the filter. (2026-03-29)

Limit: every number comes from `facts.md` or the draft. Never round up to sound
bigger.

**4. The real question (22% of posts).** A question specific enough that a
reader can answer it.

> Anyone found a way to prototype haptics without building a native app?
> (2026-04-12)

> For those testing with paper prototypes, do you still time the tasks?
> (2026-06-02)

Limit: a question is never added to a post that did not have one.

## Shapes Sam actually writes

| Shape | Length | How it opens | How it ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prototype report | 30 to 80 words | What was tried today | A result, or a question |
| Before and after | 15 to 40 words + 2 images | "Left is..." or "Before:" | Stops after the change |
| Tool tip | 50 to 120 words | The tool and the task | The setting or shortcut |
| Thread | 4 to 7 posts | The finished prototype | What Sam would skip next time |

Shapes are examples, not mandatory structures or length targets.

## Mechanics

- Punctuation: parentheses for asides, full stops for pivots, no em dashes in
  the sample, exclamation marks in 6% of posts.
- Emoji: in 12% of posts, always a single one at the end of the last line.
- Hashtags: none on the first platform, one topic tag on the second.
- Capitalisation and numbers: sentence case, numerals for every number.
- Words that are Sam's (preserve when supplied, never add as a quota): `done
  enough`, `clickable`, `scrappy`, `round` (for a test round).

## Dead register

Older agency-account copy, excluded from the corpus:

> We are delighted to unveil the new Northwind design system, a
> comprehensive suite of components crafted to empower teams. Learn more at the link below.

## Recorded preferences

**2026-09-02**: Keep the tester count in prototype reports, even when it is
small. Sam's words: "Five testers is the real number. Don't hide it." Narrows:
the rule that a report opens on the result.

**2026-08-15**: Do not delete the closing question to satisfy the
engagement-bait rule. Sam's words: "The question is the whole point of the post." If a
question is weak, move it or make it name who should answer.

**2026-07-20**: No credential-led openers. Sam's words: "Nobody cares about old job
titles. Start with the prototype." Lead with the thing that happened.

**2026-06-30**: A list post says what each item is good for. Sam's words: "A
bare list of tools is useless. One line each on why." One true line per item.

## What Sam can claim

Facts live in `facts.md`: the prototypes Sam built, tester counts per round, the
tools used and the dates. No metric, client, result or anecdote outside it.
