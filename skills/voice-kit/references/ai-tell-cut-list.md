# AI-tell cut list

The line-level pass after drafting. Apply only as far as it helps: a rough line
in the author's voice beats a clean line in nobody's. Supplied wording wins; if
the author wrote one of these on purpose, explain what is empty before cutting.

## Patterns

- **Binary contrast.** "It's not X, it's Y." / "X isn't the problem. Y is." /
  "Forget X." / "Less X, more Y." State Y directly. Keep a real comparison the
  author supplied.
- **Colon reveal.** A noun phrase, a colon, a dramatic reveal. Cut unearned
  drama, not the colon; labels like "Recipe:" are fine.
- **Faux-insight setup.** "Here's what nobody tells you", "most people get this
  wrong". Delete the setup, keep the claim. Authority comes from a receipt.
- **Throat-clearing opener.** "Here's the thing", "Let me be clear", "In
  today's world". Open on what happened.
- **Trailing significance clause.** "..., highlighting the shift toward..."
  Cut it, or say what actually follows for the reader.
- **Aphorism landing.** A closing line built to be quoted. Keep a useful
  conclusion, thanks, a real question, or stop.
- **Tricolon polish.** Three balanced fragments in a row. Once is rhythm; every
  post is a template.
- **Symmetrical paragraphs.** Equal-length paragraphs read as generated. Real
  posts run ragged.
- **Invented specificity.** A number, client or result with no source. Vague is
  recoverable; a fabricated metric in public is not.

## Words to cut when you generate them

delve, dive into, unpack, leverage, utilize, harness, foster, robust, seamless,
streamline, empower, unlock, elevate, embark, supercharge, cutting-edge, game
changer, paradigm shift, transformative, ever-evolving, landscape, realm, at its
core, testament to, in order to, straightforward, this changes everything.

Dead transitions: furthermore, additionally, moreover, moving forward, at the
end of the day, in other words, it goes without saying, it's worth noting.

Engagement bait: let that sink in, read that again, full stop, you're not ready
for this.

Dead launch register: thrilled to announce, excited to share, proud to be part
of, bring your ideas to life.

These are signals, not proof the author never says them. Preserve a supplied
word when it names a concrete benefit.

Empty when unearned, fine when spoken: just, literally, honestly, actually,
really, kinda. Keep them where they carry rhythm.

## Punctuation and format

- Do not add em dashes. Use a comma, full stop, colon or the author's own
  pivot (an ellipsis, for some people).
- Do not add a CTA, hashtag, emoji or "link in comments" the draft lacked.
- One topic per post. Offer a split when topics compete.
- A real reader question stays. If it is weak, move it or make it name who
  should answer; cutting it is the wrong fix.
- Match the context: if a quote, image or video explains it, do not inflate a
  small reaction into an essay.
