# Source and attribution

Inspired by Eric Provencher ([@pvncher](https://x.com/pvncher)),
[Rethinking skills and prompts for GPT-6 Astra](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra),
published on OpenAI Developers September 11, 2026. Read September 13, 2026.

The original inspiration was the author's
[earlier X article](https://x.com/pvncher/status/2095991462416490862), published
September 5, 2026. Read September 7, 2026, including the two instruction examples
shown as images. Those dates describe the original review, not the later OpenAI
Developers publication.

The article motivates reviewing accumulated instructions as models change:
precise skill descriptions, task-dependent access to detail, less unnecessary
prescription, selective repository context, proportionate validation, explicit
decision boundaries, and completion criteria that support continued work.
Its comparisons between model generations are the author's observations, not
universal guarantees or benchmark results established by this package.

This skill is an independently written implementation by PZA-skills, not an
official skill by Eric Provencher or OpenAI, and implies no endorsement. Local
evidence checks, audit/update modes, scope controls, approval handling, examples,
quality scoring, and the report format are implementation choices, not quotations
from the article.

The implementation is distributed under this repository's MIT license. The linked
article and its images remain their authors' material; they are not included or
relicensed here. Preserve attribution when redistributing this skill. Reading the
article online is optional at invocation time; the workflow works offline.
