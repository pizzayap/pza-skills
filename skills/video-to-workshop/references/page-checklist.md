# Page checklist

A manual version of the checks a series checker can automate. Errors must be
fixed; warnings are aims. Run it on every page, then compare pages in a series.

## Hero

- [ ] Eyebrow, series label and the four hero facts (Time, Level, You need, You
      leave with) use the series' fixed wording, in that order.
- [ ] Lede is one sentence, 40 words at most.
- [ ] Promise is one sentence, 25 words at most.
- [ ] "You need" is 2 to 5 short items and holds no ⚠️.
- [ ] "Time" reads "N minutes, P parts" and N equals the total below.

## Flow and timing

- [ ] A "Before the day" section exists, states its minutes as outside the
      total, and has a glossary.
- [ ] There are two or three parts. Every timed block sits inside a part.
- [ ] The sum of every timed block (stages plus the final "Show it") equals the
      hero total. Each part header's minutes equal the sum of its blocks.
- [ ] The last stage of every part has a "Show it" box; no other stage does.
- [ ] The last part ends with the timed "Show it" block.
- [ ] Optional extras carry their own minutes, outside the total, and a done
      checkbox.
- [ ] Any progress counter, side rail or overview strip agrees with the stage
      and part counts and the minutes.

## Each stage

- [ ] Header shows the stage number and minutes; the minutes match the block.
- [ ] Order: "You'll have", watch link, steps, figure, "Done when", "Show it" if
      last in its part, then collapsed sections.
- [ ] "From the video" and "For the facilitator" exist; the facilitator section
      is last and opens with "M min:" and the split.
- [ ] Three to six steps; each step 30 words at most (aim for 25).
- [ ] 200 open words at most outside prompts and collapsed sections (aim 180).
- [ ] No video timestamps in open text except watch links, captions and prompt
      notes.
- [ ] No definitions in brackets; terms live in the glossary.
- [ ] "Done when" heading and a "Stage N done" checkbox.

## Prompts

- [ ] Each prompt sits inside the step that sends it; one prompt per step.
- [ ] No step points at a prompt by position ("the prompt below").
- [ ] Each prompt is labeled "From the video at MM:SS" or "Workshop prompt".
- [ ] An adapted video prompt keeps the video's wording collapsed under it.
- [ ] A run line sits in the "While it runs" step, or under its prompt, with a
      maximum and a fallback.

## Figures

- [ ] Every stage has a figure; each part card has one image.
- [ ] Captions are 30 words at most (aim 25) and say what to notice.
- [ ] Each image's kind is recorded. A re-rendered frame says "redrawn"; an
      illustration says "not from the video". No visible badges.
- [ ] Images are reasonably small (under about 400 KB each).

## Claims and preservation

- [ ] Every price, plan, model name and feature is sourced and dated.
- [ ] What costs money is named, with a spending limit.
- [ ] Every ⚠️, caveat, credit and quote from the previous draft is still on the
      page or listed under "Removed on purpose" in the notes.
- [ ] Checklists are real checkboxes.
- [ ] Sources rows and footer match the series template.

## Series page (when there are several workshops)

- [ ] Each card's minutes, part count and image match its workshop page.
