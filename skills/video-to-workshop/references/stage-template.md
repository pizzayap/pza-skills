# Stage template and page skeleton

Markup-agnostic. Use whatever page format the series publishes in (HTML,
Markdown, a notes app). Keep the order and the labels identical on every page
in a series.

## Page skeleton

```text
Hero
  Eyebrow (fixed wording for the series)
  Title: names the thing and the action
  Lede: one sentence, 40 words at most
  Time: N minutes, P parts
  Level: who it is for
  You need: 2 to 5 short items (app, account or plan, optional tools and
            which cost money, setup minutes). No ⚠️ here.
  You leave with: the promise, one sentence, 25 words at most

Part cards: one per part, one image each, one goal each

Before the day (about N min, outside the total)
  [ ] checklist items people tick off
  Costs and the spending limit to set
  ⚠️ things to check before the session (plan requirements, availability)
  Words you'll meet: the glossary

Part 1: <goal> (N min)
  Stage 1 ... Stage k   (last stage ends with a Show it box)
Part 2 ...
Part 3 ...
  Final Show it block (timed), holding the reusable prompt for real work

Optional extras (N min, outside the total)
Sources: Video, Sponsor, Images, Workshop additions, Timings, Time-sensitive
Footer: status and date, method credit
```

## One stage

```text
Stage N · M min
<Title: the action>

You'll have: <the one visible result>
Watch: <link to the video at the start timestamp>

1. <One action, 30 words at most.>
2. <Send this, changing [the slot]:>
     Workshop prompt
     <prompt text with [fill-ins] marked>
     ▸ The video's wording   (collapsed, only when you adapted it)
       From the video at MM:SS
       <exact words>
     Run: up to 8 min. Not done? Stop it and continue from what exists.
3. While it runs, <a useful task>.
4. <Check or choose something.> Example: <one line, from the video if shown>

Figure: <frame> Caption: what to notice, 30 words at most.

Done when: <observable check>.   [ ] Stage N done

Show it (last stage of a part only)
  Show <the result> to a neighbor. They answer: <one question>.

▸ Stuck?               fixes for the likely failures
▸ Why                  optional; the reason behind the method
▸ From the video       timestamps used, what the video shows and does not,
                       what is workshop adaptation, short quotes, image edits
▸ For the facilitator  M min: <split, e.g. 3 to prompt, up to 8 run, 4 check>
```

## Notes file sections

- Promise, lede, audience
- Chapter map: timestamp, chapter, delivery mode, destination
- Stage map: stage, timestamps, minutes and split, run lengths seen in video
- Caption corrections
- Image provenance: file, timestamp, kind, edits
- Decisions, with dates
- Removed on purpose
- Open questions (⚠️)
